Gloop

Privacy policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

The short version

Gloop keeps your training on your phone. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no ads. We do not collect, sell or share your data.

What the app stores

Your workouts, sets, personal records, weekly plan, body stats (weight, body fat, calories), your gym buddy and your settings. All of it lives in the app's storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by us.

Permissions

Notifications show the running workout and tell you when a rest is over.

Notification access is optional and off until you turn it on for the PR soundtrack. With it, the app reads the title and artist of the song Spotify or YouTube Music is playing at the moment you set a record, and saves it next to that record on your phone. Nothing else is read and nothing leaves the phone.

Internet is declared by the app, but Gloop does not send your training data anywhere.

Links you share

When you share your PRs or a workout, the data travels inside the link you send, after the # sign. Whoever you send it to can see it. If they open it in a browser, the page on fitnessfrog.github.io (hosted by GitHub) reads the link on their device. Browsers never send the part after the # to the server, so neither we nor GitHub receive it. GitHub may log ordinary visits to the page, like any website host.

Children

Gloop is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome as an issue at github.com/FitnessFrog/FitnessFrog.github.io.
